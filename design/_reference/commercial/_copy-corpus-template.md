## Fathom AI（fathom.ai）

**定位**：
- "Fathom captures, transcribes, and summarizes Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams calls. Free for individuals, with AI-powered CRM updates for teams."
- "AI Meeting Notetaker"
- "Never Take Notes Again"

**Hero 文案**：
- 主标题："AI notetaking that is out of this world"
- 副标题："Fathom summarizes your meetings so you can focus on the conversation. Now available bot-free."
- 按钮："Get Started - Free Forever" / "Sign Up Free"
- banner："It's a whole new orbit. Announcing bot-free capture, a new desktop app, ChatGPT & Claude integrations & more"

**产品特性文案**：
- "Capture notes your way — bot or no bot — so you can stay focused on the meeting"
- "AI summaries instantly available after your call"
- "Read what mattered. Skim what didn't."
- "Ask Fathom" (the AI chat feature name)

**结构 / 节奏**：
- 极短：3-5 词
- 极口语："Never take notes again" / "out of this world" / "bot-free"
- 押头韵：Fathom / Free / Forever / Focus / Focused
- 数字：6,840 reviews, 5.0 stars

**复用判断**：
- ✅ "极口语 / 极短 / 押头韵" 节奏值得借鉴
- ❌ "out of this world" 太空感强 + "bot-free" 强调技术 —— 不适合映话
- ✅ 信任数字：映话后续可放 review 数量

## Final Round AI（finalroundai.com）

**定位**：
- "#1 AI Interview Assistant (10M+ Users)"
- "Real-time AI interview assistance during live interviews, AI mock practice, and resume tools"
- "Crack Every Interview with Real-Time AI Assistant"
- "Stealth Mode" / "Interview Copilot®"

**Hero 文案**：
- 主标题："Crack Every Interview with **Real-Time AI Assistant**"
- 副标题："Final Round AI delivers instant, undetectable answers and personalized feedback during live interviews. Practice with AI mock interviews and get real-time support when it matters most—completely invisible to interviewers."
- 按钮："Download Now" / "Sign up - It's Free!"
- chip 标签："Tailored AI Answers" / "Customization" / "Desktop App" / "Interview Report"

**产品特性文案**：
- "Your Personal AI Copilot for Every Interview"
- "Stays hidden during screen sharing"
- "Works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and HireVue"
- "Built for any online interviews"
- "Helps you answer questions in real time"

**隐身/安全文案**：
- "Invisible on dock"
- "Invisible in activity monitor"
- "Designed to operate discreetly"
- "Completely undetectable"

**FAQ 文案（部分）**：
- "What is Stealth Mode? Stealth Mode is a feature within the Final Round AI desktop application that activates during live interviews. When a meeting is detected, users can launch the Stealth Copilot to receive real-time, structured response guidance while the interview is in progress."
- "Is Final Round AI detectable? Designed to operate discreetly during live interviews. It runs through the desktop app and does not interfere with video conferencing platforms, screen sharing, audio, or camera functionality."
- "Is using an AI interview assistant legal or ethical? AI interview assistants are tools designed to help candidates prepare for and navigate interviews more effectively. Company policies regarding external tools can vary, so candidates should review the interview guidelines provided by each employer."

**结构 / 节奏**：
- 行动 + 利益："Crack every interview" / "Get real-time support"
- 信任锚点："10M+ users" / "4.9 stars / 296 reviews"
- 隐身叙事反复强调：invisible / undetectable / discreetly / hidden / Stealth
- FAQ 承担"打消顾虑"角色——主要顾虑是合法性

**复用判断**：
- ✅ "隐身"叙事模式——映话要走"低调 / 不打扰 / 不抢戏" 的路子，而不是强调"反检测"
- ✅ "Crack / Personal AI Copilot / Real-Time" 等高频词节奏
- ❌ "undetectable" 这种词**绝对不能用**——太灰色
- ✅ 信任锚点：映话后续可以做"完全本地"作为信任叙事
