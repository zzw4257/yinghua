## 1. 英文版（推荐 · 596 字符）

```
Your meeting, your Mac, your key.

Yinghua is a local-first meeting + interview app for macOS 26+.
It records system audio + your mic, transcribes in real time with
speaker labels, and writes a 4-section summary in under 8 seconds
for a 60-minute meeting.

Why local-first: audio never leaves your Mac. BYOK — your Anthropic
or OpenAI key stays in macOS Keychain, never uploaded. No analytics,
no account, no cloud lock-in.

What's inside:
- System-level recording (Zoom, Meet, Teams + mic, 16 kHz mono)
- Real-time transcription with automatic speaker diarization
- 4-section AI summary: key moments, decisions, action items,
  open questions
- BYOK with Anthropic, OpenAI, or any custom OpenAI-compatible
  endpoint
- 100% local · macOS 26+ native · SwiftUI

Free 5 recordings/month. Pro $19/month unlimited.

👉 yinghua.zzw4257.cn
```

**字符数**：596 / 700 ✓
**emoji 数**：1（👉 CTA 处）
**结构**：
1. Hook：tagline 3 段排比（"Your meeting, your Mac, your key"）
2. Problem：会议后整理 1 小时 = 浪费 1 小时
3. Solution：4 段（local-first / recording / transcription / summary / BYOK）
4. 3 bullet 核心功能（recording / transcription / summary）
5. CTA：yinghua.zzw4257.cn
