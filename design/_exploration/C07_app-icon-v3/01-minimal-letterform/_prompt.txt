## THE Y MARK (must be IDENTICAL across all 3 variants)

The letterform is a custom "Y" with these geometric rules:
- The Y is composed of exactly 2 strokes: a left diagonal stroke from top-left going down-right, and a right diagonal stroke from top-right going down-left, meeting at the center, then continuing as a single vertical stroke down to the bottom.
- All strokes have rounded ends (semicircular caps), ~16% of letter height thick.
- The Y is centered both horizontally and vertically inside the icon's safe area (with ~22% margin on all sides).
- The Y's overall height = ~52% of icon's inner height.
- The Y's overall width = ~52% of icon's inner width.
- The strokes are slightly tapered: thicker at the meeting point, slightly thinner at the ends.
- No serifs, no flourishes, no additional dots, no speech bubble, no infinity loop. Just the Y.
